FAQ

Good to know.

Are the displayed prices live? +

No. Prices are documented references to genuine published product listings. They may have changed since collection. Confirm your own sale price and inventory before offering orders.

Can I place a real order here? +

Not yet. This is an interactive storefront prototype. Cart and wishlist are saved on your device; payment, order processing and shipping have not been enabled.

Are these product photographs? +

No. The original images here are clearly marked preview illustrations. Replace them with authentic, licensed product photography before launch.

Is KANEMI-DE an official anime franchise partner? +

No official partnership is claimed. Character and anime names belong to their respective rights holders. Brand and merchandise claims require independent verification.
